International buyer checklist

  1. Obtain codice fiscale and Italian bank account early.
  2. Preliminary (compromesso) review by independent notary/lawyer.
  3. Cadastral plan must match physical layout.
  4. Energy certificate (APE) and condominium minutes.
  5. Budget 10–12% on top of price for taxes/fees (varies by seller type and incentives).
