ENGLISH PROPERTY FAQ

Clear answers.
Before your next step.

Practical questions about selling, buying and letting in Padova and its province. These are starting points for a conversation, not legal, tax, credit or investment advice.

Answers checked against the Italian FAQ themes on 24 November 2026. Individual legal, tax and finance questions need case-specific professional advice.

Working with Righetto

Where does Righetto provide local property services?

Righetto Immobiliare is based in Limena and works in Padova and its province. Explain your preferred area and intended use so the team can confirm the scope of assistance. The regional pages are a starting point for discussion, not a promise of listings or offices across Italy.

Who should I speak to about sales and lettings?

Ginevra Capon is the contact for the International project. Gino handles sales and commercial lettings; Linda handles residential and student lettings. Introduce the purpose of your enquiry and your preferred language so the appropriate contact can be agreed.

How are estate agency fees agreed?

The agency does not publish a standard fee online. Ask for the services, fees, payment conditions and responsibilities to be explained and agreed in writing for your instruction. Do not infer a price or included service from a general description.

Can I request a property valuation without committing to sell?

The Italian site offers a free valuation without an obligation to instruct the agency. Describe the property type, approximate area, condition and locality. Confirm the scope of the valuation and the information needed; an initial estimate should not be treated as a guaranteed selling price.

Selling a property

What does the selling service involve?

The Italian site describes valuation, photography and virtual tours, marketing, viewing management and assistance through negotiation and completion. Confirm the actual package and responsibilities in your instruction. Specialist legal and technical work needs the appropriate appointed professional.

How long will it take to sell my property?

There is no guaranteed sale period. Price, condition, location, documents, demand and the buyer’s position can affect the timetable. Ask for an assessment of your property and the assumptions behind any estimate, rather than relying on a general number of days.

Which documents should a seller prepare?

Start with the ownership deed, cadastral information and plan, energy certificate and the available planning, building and property records. The exact checks and documents depend on the property and transaction. Ask your notary and appointed technical adviser to identify gaps before a commitment.

What are a virtual tour and an APE?

A virtual tour lets someone explore images of a property remotely; it does not replace a viewing or technical inspection. An APE is the Italian energy performance certificate. Ask a qualified technician about the requirements, validity and cost for the property. No contact uplift or standard certification price is promised here.

Buying in Italy

What should I do before signing an offer?

Define your total budget and appoint your own advisers early. The Italian Notariat recommends seeking notarial advice before signing a purchase proposal or preliminary agreement. Clarify conditions, deadlines, checks, deposits and what becomes binding before you sign.

Can a foreign citizen buy property in Italy?

Eligibility depends on the buyer’s circumstances. The Italian Notariat explains that checks may involve nationality, residence status and reciprocity rules where applicable. Ask your chosen notary to confirm the rules for you before making a binding commitment. Do not assume that one rule applies to every overseas buyer.

What costs come on top of the price?

Allow for the applicable purchase taxes, notarial fees, agreed agency fees, professional checks and any finance or property work costs. Taxes depend on the transaction and eligibility for relief. Obtain a case-specific written breakdown from your advisers; this FAQ does not quote universal rates or fixed professional fees.

Can I claim first-home tax relief?

First-home relief depends on statutory conditions relating to the buyer, the property and the transaction. Ask the notary or tax adviser to confirm eligibility and any steps or deadlines for your case. Age, overseas residence or a first purchase alone does not establish entitlement.

What is the difference between an offer, a preliminary agreement and completion?

These are different stages of a transaction, with different legal effects. A purchase proposal or preliminary agreement can create binding obligations before the final notarial deed. Have your notary explain the document, conditions, registration and any protection needed before signing.

How should I treat a deposit or advance payment?

The wording and legal purpose of a payment matter. A caparra and an acconto are not interchangeable labels. Ask your notary or legal adviser to explain the payment, consequences of default, protections and any conditions before transferring money. No standard deposit percentage is assumed.

Can I discuss an auction purchase or inherited property?

Introduce the case and ask the team to confirm whether and how it can assist. Auctions and inherited property need their own document, title and procedural checks with the relevant professionals. Do not assume a discount, a clear property, a simple timetable or a particular service package.

Mortgages and finance

Is a mortgage guaranteed, including a low-deposit or 100% mortgage?

No. Lending is decided by the lender after reviewing the borrower, affordability, property and its own criteria. A valuation, guarantee scheme or early conversation is not approval. Resolve finance conditions with your lender and advisers before signing a purchase commitment.

How should I compare mortgage offers?

Compare the written terms, total cost, fixed or variable rate, fees, insurance requirements, repayment conditions and risks. Ask the lender or qualified credit adviser to explain TAN and TAEG, any rate cap, refinancing or early repayment terms. This page does not publish current rates or recommend a loan.

What documents and time should I allow for finance?

Ask the lender for its requirements for your income, identity, liabilities, residence and property. Overseas or self-employed income may need additional review. Timings are lender- and case-specific. Coordinate any finance condition and completion deadline with the lender and notary.

Are under-36 relief and the Consap guarantee the same thing?

No. Purchase-tax relief and the First Home Guarantee Fund are separate measures with different conditions. Do not assume an age-based exemption or a guarantee means that a mortgage will be granted. Confirm the current scheme, eligibility and lender participation with the relevant official source and your advisers.

Letting and ownership

Which rental agreement is appropriate?

The right agreement depends on the intended use, parties, duration and applicable rules. Ask the agency and your adviser about the requirements for the specific case, including any agreed-rent, temporary or student arrangement. Do not select a contract or tax regime solely from a brief online summary.

How are a tenancy deposit and unpaid rent handled?

The deposit, payment terms and responsibilities need to be set out correctly in the tenancy agreement. If rent is unpaid or there is a dispute, obtain case-specific legal advice. Neither a deposit nor tenant screening removes all risk, and this page promises no eviction timetable.

Can I use a property for short-term or tourist letting?

Do not assume that a property is eligible or that a general rental rule is enough. Check the intended activity, property, building rules and current national, regional and municipal obligations with the relevant advisers and authorities. Obtain a complete cost and compliance assessment before relying on rental income.

Value, returns and taxes

What return or future price can I expect?

Neither a return nor a future sale price is guaranteed. Test income assumptions against vacancy, management, maintenance, taxes, finance and purchase or sale costs. Ask for dated comparable evidence and the basis of any estimate. This FAQ does not repeat unsourced area prices or standard yields.

How do I check IMU, capital gains, inheritance or renovation relief?

These depend on the property, owner, transaction, works and current rules. Ask a qualified tax adviser and, where relevant, the notary and municipality for a case-specific assessment. Do not rely on a generic percentage, exemption or deadline without confirming that it applies to you.
