THE PAGE AT A GLANCE

What do you need to do?

Choose the route that matches your plans. The scope of each service is agreed before work begins.

01

Sell a property

Start with a discussion of the property and your objectives. Valuation, presentation, viewings and negotiations form the sales route, with the work organised around the individual property.

02

Buy a property

Define the area, property type, budget and intended use. Use the available information to compare options, plan viewings and identify questions to settle before making a commitment.

03

Let a property

Discuss the intended letting, the property and the tenant profile. Our published services include tenant search, tenancy agreements, registration and management of contractual deadlines.

04

Manage a property

For owners who need ongoing support, discuss tenant communication, payment monitoring and coordination of maintenance. Agree responsibilities and reporting before appointing a manager.

05

Plan a commercial move

For a shop, office or other business premises, begin with the activity and the space it needs. Property selection and any technical checks must follow those requirements, rather than appearance alone.

06

Prepare the paperwork

Property documents, proposed terms and questions for the notary need attention before signing. Identify missing information early and agree which checks belong to the agency and which require a qualified specialist.

CLEAR RESPONSIBILITIES

Know who is doing what.

A purchase, sale or tenancy can involve a notary, technical professional, tax adviser or lender. Discuss which appointments and checks are needed for your case. Estate agency assistance does not replace independent legal, tax or technical advice.

Fees, external costs and the scope of any appointment should be clear before you proceed. This page is a service overview, not a quotation or a promise that a particular outcome is possible.

A PERSONAL POINT OF CONTACT

Tell us what needs to happen.

A property reference or a short description of your plans is enough to start the conversation. Mention your preferred language and the area involved.
