START A CONVERSATION

Start with your plans.

Looking for a home, planning a sale or considering a professional collaboration? Tell us the purpose of your enquiry, the area involved and your preferred language.

What helps us understand your enquiry?

  • For a property already seen: its reference code and your questions.
  • For a search: area, property type, intended use and an indicative budget.
  • For a sale or letting: municipality and a short description. Do not send private documents at this stage.
  • For a collaboration: your role, country and the kind of support you are seeking.

A short description is enough for the first contact. Do not include identity documents, tax records, bank details or access credentials.

Choose how to contact the agency

Use your own email or phone app to introduce your plans. Opening a link does not send a message or place a call automatically. Include a property reference if you have one; do not include identity documents or confidential records in a first enquiry.
