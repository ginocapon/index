How can property professionals start a discussion?

Introduce your professional role, location and the specific property or client question. A conversation does not itself create a partnership, instruction or exclusivity. Technical, legal, tax and finance work must remain within the scope of the qualified professionals appointed for the case.

THE PAGE AT A GLANCE

Start with the details that matter.

Clear questions make the next conversation more useful.

01

Explain your role

Introduce your professional activity, location and the kind of enquiry you would like to discuss. Do not assume that sending an introduction creates a partnership or an instruction.

02

Bring a defined question

A client brief, a property reference or a specific operational need gives the discussion a purpose. Keep personal and confidential documents out of the first enquiry.

03

Separate professional responsibilities

Technical, legal, tax and finance work must be assigned to appropriately qualified professionals. Each appointment and its scope should be clear to the client.

04

Agree the next step

If there is a suitable basis to continue, discuss responsibilities, information sharing and commercial terms before work begins. No referral terms or exclusivity are implied here.

A PERSONAL POINT OF CONTACT

Tell us what you have in mind.

Introduce your enquiry and the area involved. Scope, availability and any professional appointments can then be discussed for the individual case.
