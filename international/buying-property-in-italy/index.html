BUYING PROPERTY IN ITALY

Understand the next step before you commit.

A property search is only part of a purchase. Your circumstances, the property documents and the wording of any agreement matter before you sign or pay a deposit.

What should a buyer clarify before signing in Italy?

Clarify your overall budget, the proposed agreement and the checks needed for your circumstances and the property. The Italian Notariat recommends involving your chosen notary early, before signing a purchase proposal or preliminary agreement. An offer should not be treated as a casual reservation.

THE PAGE AT A GLANCE

Questions worth settling early.

A practical checklist for your discussion, not a fixed promise about the property or outcome.

01

Define the brief

Begin with how you intend to use the property, the location, your overall budget and any financing needs. Separate essential requirements from preferences. A viewing shortlist is more useful when these points are clear.

02

Choose your advisers early

The Italian notary is an independent public official, not the seller’s representative. The buyer chooses the notary. The Italian Notariat recommends seeking advice at the start of negotiations, before signing a proposal or preliminary agreement.

03

Check your individual position

Do not assume that the same requirements apply to every foreign buyer. Ask the notary to review nationality, residence status, the proposed transaction and any relevant reciprocity conditions for your case.

04

Read before making an offer

A purchase proposal is not simply a reservation. A signed offer and its acceptance can create binding obligations. Have the terms, conditions, payment arrangements and deadlines reviewed before committing.

05

Review the property documents

Ask which title, cadastral, planning and mortgage-register checks are needed. Agree who is responsible for each review and whether a technical inspection is required. Photographs and a floor plan are not a substitute for those checks.

06

Budget beyond the asking price

Purchase taxes depend on the seller, the transaction and any relief for which the buyer qualifies. Include notarial and agency fees, specialist advice, financing and any planned work. Obtain a case-specific estimate rather than relying on a headline percentage.
