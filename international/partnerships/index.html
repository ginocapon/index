INTERNATIONAL PARTNERSHIPS

A local conversation for your clients in Italy.

If you are an estate agency abroad with a client considering Padova or its province, begin with the client’s brief. A useful collaboration starts with clear roles, permission to share information and realistic expectations.

INTERNATIONAL PARTNERSHIPSTogether.Visual composition · not a property or project

Can an overseas agency introduce a client enquiry?

An overseas estate agency can introduce a defined enquiry concerning Padova or its province. Explain the brief without sending confidential client documents initially. Scope, responsibilities, information sharing and commercial terms need agreement before work starts. An introduction does not create a referral agreement.

THE PAGE AT A GLANCE

Start with the details that matter.

Clear questions make the next conversation more useful.

01

Introduce the enquiry

Tell us your agency, country and the type of request. Share the location, property requirements and indicative budget without sending identity documents or confidential client records in the first message.

02

Agree the scope

Discuss who will communicate with the client, arrange viewings and follow up. Local availability and the work required must be checked before any commitment is made to the client.

03

Set terms before proceeding

Referral arrangements, responsibilities, fees and the treatment of client information need to be agreed between the parties. This page does not create an agency agreement or referral entitlement.

04

Keep the client informed

Avoid promising availability, financing, legal eligibility or a completion date before the relevant checks. Record the agreed next step and who is responsible for it.
