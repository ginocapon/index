A local starting point for buyers and owners based in the United States. Righetto Immobiliare is based in Limena and works in Padova and its province.

What can you discuss with us?

Introduce a home search, sale, residential letting, commercial property requirement or professional enquiry. The team will confirm the scope, responsibilities, fees and next steps for the individual instruction. We do not promise a sale date, investment return or loan approval.

Before you make a commitment

Speak with your own Italian notary before signing a purchase proposal or preliminary agreement. Nationality, residence and the transaction can affect the checks needed. Ask your notary to confirm your own position, documents, tax treatment and any language or representation arrangements.

Your overall budget

Allow for the purchase price, taxes, notarial and agreed agency fees, professional checks, finance, works and ongoing ownership costs. US and Italian tax or reporting obligations require advice appropriate to your circumstances. No individual tax outcome is assumed.

Who will receive your enquiry?

Ginevra Capon is the contact for the International project. Gino handles sales and commercial lettings; Linda handles residential and student lettings. The team’s recorded languages include English and Spanish; this page does not promise support in every language.

How to start

Tell us your aim, preferred locality, indicative budget and any property reference. Do not send identity documents, bank records or confidential client files in a first message. The email and phone links open your own apps; you decide whether to send or call.

About the selection

The English property selection is a dated catalogue snapshot, not a live availability feed. The map is illustrative and no exact property address is displayed.
