Italian property terms: questions behind the words

An English-language introduction to proposta, preliminare, rogito, caparra, acconto, APE and cadastral terms, with questions for your advisers.

Righetto International · Sources checked 24 November 2026 · General property information

Proposta and preliminare

A proposta d’acquisto is a purchase proposal; a preliminare is a preliminary agreement. These are not casual expressions of interest simply because completion comes later. The Italian Notariat warns that they can create binding commitments and recommends involving your chosen notary before signing. Ask what the document requires, which conditions protect the intended plan and what happens if those conditions are not met.

Rogito and notaio

The rogito is the final notarial deed in the purchase process. The notaio is the notary, an independent public official responsible for the legal checks within that role. The Notariat states that the purchaser is free to choose the notary; the bank or estate agent should not impose one. Ask the notary to explain the steps, costs, timing and documents needed for your own transaction.

Caparra and acconto

These terms describe payments with different legal purposes. Do not assume that every deposit is refundable or that a fixed percentage is mandatory. Ask your notary or legal adviser to identify the payment in the document, its consequences if the transaction fails, any agreed conditions and the appropriate payment arrangements. An English translation of one word is not a review of the contract.

Catasto, planimetria and APE

Catasto refers to the cadastral system; a planimetria catastale is a cadastral plan. These are part of the information professionals review, not a substitute for every planning, building, title or condition check. An APE is the energy performance certificate. Ask a qualified professional what records and checks are required and whether any document needs updating for this property.

Prima casa and prezzo-valore

Prima casa names a tax-relief framework with conditions, not simply any property you personally call your first home. Prezzo-valore is a tax-base mechanism that can apply under specified conditions. The Revenue Agency explains that the seller, transaction and buyer’s circumstances matter. Have the notary or tax adviser confirm eligibility and calculations instead of applying a label from a listing to your own tax position.

Use the glossary to ask better questions

Keep the original Italian wording alongside any translation in your notes, then ask the responsible adviser to explain its effect in the actual document. If you are not comfortable with the language, raise interpretation and document-understanding needs before any signature. This glossary introduces questions; it does not certify a translation or give legal advice.
