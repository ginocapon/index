INTERNATIONAL JOURNAL · PROPERTY PLANNING

Preparing a property for sale in Padova

A seller’s preparation checklist covering objectives, records, presentation, access and the written agency instruction, without guaranteed sale times.

Righetto International · Sources checked 24 November 2026 · General property information

Agree the objective before the presentation

Start with what the sale needs to achieve and any practical constraints. Explain whether someone occupies the property, what access can be arranged and which dates matter to you. Ask how an initial valuation will be supported and which comparable evidence is relevant. An estimate is not a guaranteed price or a promise that the sale will complete within a set number of days.

Bring the available records together

Gather the ownership documents, cadastral information and plans, energy certificate and available building or planning records. Ask the appointed professionals which checks and updates are needed for this property. Keep missing information visible rather than assuming that an attractive listing solves it. The Italian Notariat recommends involving the notary early in the transaction; technical checks may need a separate qualified professional.

Present the property accurately

Righetto’s published selling services include photography, virtual tours, marketing, viewing management and assistance through negotiation and completion. Confirm the package that actually applies to your instruction. Presentation should help a buyer understand the property, not conceal condition, invent a possible use or imply that unapproved work is feasible. Images and virtual tours do not replace inspections and document review.

Plan viewing access and communications

Agree how visits will be arranged and what information you expect after them. Tell the agency about occupiers, pets or practical access constraints that affect appointments. Do not publish confidential documents or unnecessary personal details in a listing. A clear process makes it easier to compare feedback with the price, condition and type of buyer being reached.

Put the instruction in writing

Ask for the scope of service, fees, conditions, duration and responsibilities to be explained before you instruct the agency. The Italian site does not publish a standard agency fee. Confirm what specialist work is included or separate. Review the plan when reliable evidence changes, rather than treating marketing activity alone as proof that the price or timetable is right.
