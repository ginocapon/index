Inheriting a home in Padova: succession steps and selling it

Organise the succession file, ownership checks and possible sale with professional help, especially when heirs live abroad.

Gino Capon · Adapted from the Italian article · Editorial revision 25 November 2026

In short

Ask the notary to identify the applicable succession rules, the people entitled and any cross-border issues. Do not assume that possession of the keys proves ownership or that every estate needs the same documents.

Start with the estate and the people involved

Collect the death certificate, available will, family information, property records, debts and previous deeds. Ask the notary to identify the applicable succession rules, the people entitled and any cross-border issues. Do not assume that possession of the keys proves ownership or that every estate needs the same documents.

Check the filing deadline and route

The Revenue Agency’s guidance sets out when the succession declaration must be filed and how. The ordinary filing period is 12 months from the opening of the succession; ask the professional handling the estate how it applies and whether any exception or special filing route is relevant. Do not rely on the claim that every case can only be filed online.

Separate declaration, acceptance and title

A succession declaration is not a substitute for checking acceptance of inheritance and the title needed for a sale. Ask the notary about acceptance, its recording, cadastral updates, co-heirs and any required authorisations. A formal division is not automatically required merely because there is more than one heir.

Before deciding to sell

Request the ownership and technical file, check registered entries and clarify who must sign. Agree how costs, use and sale decisions will be handled among those involved. If a representative signs from abroad, have the notary approve the form and scope of the power of attorney in advance.

Taxes and professional estimates

Ask for an estate-specific calculation of inheritance and property-related taxes and any relief. Do not infer that the tax position on a later sale is settled solely by the word inherited: have the adviser assess the property’s history and applicable rules. Keep the filings, deeds, invoices and receipts together.
