Rental yield in Padova: how to work out what you really earn

Use a transparent cash calculation to compare a rental plan, without turning an illustrative figure into a promised return.

Gino Capon · Adapted from the Italian article · Editorial revision 25 November 2026

In short

Record the price and all acquisition costs, then any initial works and furnishing. Decide whether you are comparing rent to purchase price alone or to the full cash invested, and label that choice. Different definitions cannot be compared as if they measured the same return.

Start with the right denominator

Record the price and all acquisition costs, then any initial works and furnishing. Decide whether you are comparing rent to purchase price alone or to the full cash invested, and label that choice. Different definitions cannot be compared as if they measured the same return.

Separate expected rent and collected rent

List the rent actually collectible under the proposed lawful contract and allow for vacant periods, arrears and collection costs. An advertised rent does not establish what your property will achieve. Record the evidence and its date rather than borrowing a neighbourhood average.

Subtract the costs you bear

Include tax, IMU where due, non-recoverable condominium charges, insurance, maintenance, management and accounting. Keep major works and replacement reserves visible. Ask the tax adviser to calculate your position instead of deriving it from a generic flat-tax example.

Show financing separately

Loan interest, fees and repayments affect cash flow. Distinguish the operating result from the amount left after financing and capital expenditure. A property can have positive rent before debt service yet still require additional cash from its owner.

Test downside cases

Recalculate with lower rent, a longer vacancy, delayed payment or an unexpected repair. Keep each assumption visible and compare the same period across alternatives. This article provides a method, not a Padova yield ranking, standard monthly rent or guaranteed investment result.
