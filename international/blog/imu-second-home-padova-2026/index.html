IMU on a second home in Padova in 2026: calculation and deadlines

Check whether IMU applies, use the correct municipality and year, and have the calculation based on the actual property and ownership.

Gino Capon · Adapted from the Italian article · Editorial revision 25 November 2026

In short

Padova’s official 2026 page confirms that the 2025 rates and deductions continue for 2026. That statement applies to Padova, not automatically to every nearby municipality. Follow the official rate prospectus and guidance for the place where the property is located.

Use the official municipal source

Padova’s official 2026 page confirms that the 2025 rates and deductions continue for 2026. That statement applies to Padova, not automatically to every nearby municipality. Follow the official rate prospectus and guidance for the place where the property is located.

Check who owes the tax and for which period

The municipality explains that the tax is assessed by calendar year and, for co-owners, by ownership share and months of possession. Main-home treatment, property category, rights held and special cases matter. Do not assume that every owned home is taxable or that calling a property a first home proves exemption.

Prepare the calculation inputs

Collect the cadastral identification and income, property category, ownership share, period of possession and use. Ask the adviser to apply the correct base, coefficient, rate, deduction or reduction. This article deliberately gives no worked annual tax that hides an unverified rate.

Payment dates in 2026

The official Padova page lists the first instalment by 16 June 2026 and the balance by 16 December 2026. The balance settles the full-year tax after the advance. Check the payment route, codes and any declaration duty using the current official guidance or a qualified adviser.

Changes and late payments

Tell the adviser about a purchase, sale, letting, change of use or any potential relief. The municipality provides guidance and an official calculator, including routes for correcting omitted or late payments. Do not copy last year’s amount without checking the inputs and your current circumstances.
