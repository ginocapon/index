The full cost of owning a home in Padova: buying, holding, selling

Build a buying, holding and selling budget so the purchase price does not hide the long-term commitments.

Gino Capon · Adapted from the Italian article · Editorial revision 25 November 2026

In short

List the purchase price, taxes, notary, agency fee, financing, checks and any initial works. Obtain quotations for your own transaction and ask what is included. The seller’s status and your eligibility for relief can change the tax treatment; a generic estimate is not a binding calculation.

Buying: separate price and transaction costs

List the purchase price, taxes, notary, agency fee, financing, checks and any initial works. Obtain quotations for your own transaction and ask what is included. The seller’s status and your eligibility for relief can change the tax treatment; a generic estimate is not a binding calculation.

Holding: plan regular and exceptional costs

Budget for condominium charges, utilities, insurance, maintenance, TARI and IMU where due. Use the current municipal guidance and the building accounts. Keep approved extraordinary works separate from routine upkeep and ask the notary how outstanding charges are allocated between seller and buyer.

Letting: measure cash retained

Rent received is not all income retained. Deduct the costs you bear, tax, management and vacant periods. Keep financing and capital works visible. If you compare different rental models, check that each is lawful for the property and uses the same cost assumptions.

Selling: check the tax position early

Do not assume every sale gain is taxed at a single flat rate or that holding a home beyond the ordinary period always removes all issues. The Revenue Agency describes a separate Superbonus-related capital-gain regime. Ask an accountant and notary to assess the history, exclusions and available treatment before setting the sale timetable.

Keep three evidence folders

Retain the purchase deed and costs, the ownership bills and works invoices, then the documents prepared for sale. Update the budget when the mortgage, building condition or intended use changes. A total-cost comparison is useful only when its assumptions remain visible.

General information, not individual legal, tax, technical or financial advice. Adapted with editorial corrections. Check the current rules and the documents with qualified advisers before making a commitment.
