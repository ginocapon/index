The energy certificate (APE) when buying a home in Padova

Read the energy certificate with a qualified professional and compare it with the property, not only with a letter in an advert.

Gino Capon · Adapted from the Italian article · Editorial revision 25 November 2026

In short

The Attestato di Prestazione Energetica reports assessed energy performance using the applicable certification method. The national guidelines include the certificate format and the indicator for commercial advertisements. Ask a qualified certifier which certificate and obligations apply to the property and transaction.

What the APE is for

The Attestato di Prestazione Energetica reports assessed energy performance using the applicable certification method. The national guidelines include the certificate format and the indicator for commercial advertisements. Ask a qualified certifier which certificate and obligations apply to the property and transaction.

Read the document, not only the class

Check the property identification, issue date, certifier, performance indicators, systems and recommendations. Ask whether the certificate is still valid and whether works or other circumstances require an update. A letter alone cannot establish your future utility bill.

Compare it with the actual home

Ask the technician to compare the certificate with the real building, systems and available records. If the property has changed, do not infer the answer from a photograph or a cadastral plan alone. Request an explanation and any necessary correction from the qualified professional.

Budget for comfort and works

Look at the building envelope, heating and cooling, controls, ventilation and summer shading. Obtain property-specific quotations and consider how you will use the home. Past bills can inform the discussion but do not guarantee your consumption, comfort or costs.

Avoid automatic forecasts

Do not assume a particular energy class guarantees faster resale, a bank loan or a fixed legal renovation deadline. Ask the bank about its assessment and the technician about current rules. This guide makes no unsupported prediction about future requirements for an individual home.

General information, not individual legal, tax, technical or financial advice. Adapted with editorial corrections. Check the current rules and the documents with qualified advisers before making a commitment.
