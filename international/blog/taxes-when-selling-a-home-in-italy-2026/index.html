Taxes when you sell a home in Padova: capital gain and what the seller pays

Ask about the property’s full history before assuming whether a sale gain is taxable or which treatment applies.

Gino Capon · Adapted from the Italian article · Editorial revision 25 November 2026

In short

A sale price above the purchase price is not enough on its own to establish the tax due. Ask the accountant and notary to review how and when the property was acquired, its use, the seller’s status and the works carried out. Keep the deeds, invoices, tax records and evidence of use.

Start with the actual transaction

A sale price above the purchase price is not enough on its own to establish the tax due. Ask the accountant and notary to review how and when the property was acquired, its use, the seller’s status and the works carried out. Keep the deeds, invoices, tax records and evidence of use.

Do not stop at the ordinary holding period

The ordinary private-sale rules have conditions and exclusions. In addition, the Revenue Agency explains a separate regime for disposals involving Superbonus works completed within the preceding ten years, introduced from 2024. Do not assume that passing the ordinary five-year period settles every case.

Have exemptions and costs checked

Residence, use as a main home, inheritance and other circumstances must be assessed under the applicable rule, not just a label used in conversation. The deductibility of works and other costs, including costs linked to tax incentives, also needs a case-specific check.

Compare payment options before the deed

Ask whether ordinary income-tax treatment or an available substitute-tax option applies to your gain, how each is calculated and when an election must be made. This article gives no implied tax rate through a worked euro example and does not recommend one option without your full circumstances.

Budget the other selling costs

Separate potential gain tax from agency fees, documents, technical work, loan arrangements and other costs you may bear. Ask the bank and notary how an outstanding mortgage will be handled. Do not assume every mortgage cancellation needs the same paid notarial procedure or that buyer and seller costs are identical across transactions.
