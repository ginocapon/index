House prices in Padova by area in 2026

A practical way to compare Padova districts and nearby municipalities without treating a broad price band as a valuation.

Gino Capon · Adapted from the Italian article · Editorial revision 25 November 2026

In short

Start with the streets and services that matter to you, not only the name of a district. Check the actual journey, noise, access, parking and nearby services at different times. Homes described with the same neighbourhood name can have very different surroundings.

Define the comparison area

Start with the streets and services that matter to you, not only the name of a district. Check the actual journey, noise, access, parking and nearby services at different times. Homes described with the same neighbourhood name can have very different surroundings.

Build a comparable set

Compare properties of similar size, condition, floor, lift access, outdoor space and energy performance. Record the date and whether a figure is an asking price or a completed sale. Do not combine those categories without explaining the difference.

Look beyond the headline price

A cheaper home can require works, higher running costs or further document checks. Request current quotations rather than assuming a standard renovation allowance. Compare the complete budget, including transaction costs and financing, before deciding that one area offers better value.

Ask for evidence before relying on a range

This guide does not publish district price bands or a city average because those require a dated, traceable dataset and a clear method. Ask the adviser to show the source, period, property category and limitations of any price comparison used for your home.

Can a price per square metre decide the purchase?

No single ratio captures condition, usable layout, title, technical compliance or your needs. Use it as one comparison tool alongside a visit, the documents and qualified advice on the specific property.
