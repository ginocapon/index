The purchase offer and price negotiation in Padova

How a written purchase offer works in Italy, what it must contain and how to negotiate in a competitive Padova market.

G Translated from the Italian article by Gino Capon · Italian original, legal references checked 11 October 2026 · 5 min read

In short

The purchase offer is the written document in which the buyer offers price and conditions to the seller. The contract is concluded when the person making the offer learns of the acceptance, within the terms of the offer (Civil Code article 1326). The confirmatory deposit (article 1385) follows acceptance. This article covers the whole negotiation; our article on the confirmatory deposit covers that instrument in depth.

What the purchase offer is and when you need it

The purchase offer is the act by which the buyer tells the seller they want to buy a property on certain conditions: price, payment method, a deadline for acceptance and any conditions precedent.

In Padova, in a market with demand for three-room flats in Limena and one-bedroom flats in the semi-centre, a complete written offer separates a serious buyer from someone negotiating verbally with no financial qualification. It does not replace the preliminary contract or the deed: it is the first formal commitment in the chain.

Legal basis: binding offer and deposit

Article 1326 of the Civil Code says the contract is concluded when the person making the offer learns of the other party’s acceptance. The acceptance must arrive within the deadline set; the offeror may treat a late acceptance as valid by notifying the other party immediately. A reply that does not match is a new offer. A deadline to accept alone does not make the offer irrevocable: article 1329 concerns a commitment to keep the offer open for a set time, during which withdrawal has no effect. Ask a professional to check the wording of the offer and the effect of its clauses.

Under Civil Code article 1385, if the party who paid a confirmatory deposit defaults, the other party may withdraw and retain it. If the recipient defaults, the non-defaulting party may withdraw and claim double. Other remedies follow different rules. This is not a general right to change your mind.

Essential elements of a written offer

An effective offer includes: identification of the property (address and cadastral data: sheet, parcel, subunit), the price offered, payment terms (deposit, balance at the deed), the acceptance deadline, conditions precedent (mortgage, valuation), an indicative deed date, and the details of buyer and seller.

Clauses on planning compliance, condominium costs and the actual state protect a prudent buyer. Cross-check with the checklist before the preliminary contract.

Price negotiation in the Padova market

The listing price is the seller’s declaration and the starting point. The analysis uses OMI values from the Revenue Agency (minimum, medium and maximum bands by zone and type), recent comparables and the state of repair. Every negotiation is individual.

When there are competing offers, compare their price, financing, conditions and timing. The position depends on the specific property and seller.

The mortgage condition: orderly practice

If you need a mortgage, have a professional draft the financing condition, including the amount, deadline and consequences if it is not met.

Presenting a bank pre-approval or a broker’s letter with the offer strengthens credibility. The bank valuation can come in below the agreed price, so a clause on the valuation result is prudent.

Acceptance deadline and counter-offer

Set a clear acceptance deadline after taking advice. The legal effect of expiry, a late reply or an extension depends on the offer and the applicable rules.

The seller’s counter-offer (different price or terms) is a new offer, and the buyer decides whether to accept. For properties of significant value there is no tacit acceptance: an express reply is needed.

A competitive market: Padova and the belt

When several buyers converge on the same property, the seller compares price, deposit, deed timing, mortgage strength and the absence of excessive conditions. A “clean” offer with a standard mortgage condition and a feasible deed date is often preferred to a higher but uncertain one.

The agency’s role in the negotiation

The agency mediates between buyer and seller: it delivers the written offer, collects the counter-offer and coordinates the timing of the deposit and the preliminary contract. The agency fee is agreed in the office. Document transparency before the offer (search, floor plan, energy certificate, condominium costs) is important.

Common mistakes in the offer and negotiation

  • A verbal offer with no written confirmation: a risk of misunderstanding.
  • An offer without a mortgage pre-approval on a property to be financed.
  • No acceptance deadline on an offer meant to be binding.
  • Ignoring approved extraordinary condominium decisions in the price offered.
  • Asking for an aggressive discount at the first viewing without conviction.
  • Signing an offer without reading the deposit and withdrawal clause.

From yes to the confirmatory deposit

Once the offer is accepted, the parties pay the confirmatory deposit: an agreed amount, traceable payment, a receipt. Then the preliminary contract is registered, or the deed follows directly, as agreed with the notary.

The financing condition and the bank’s release of funds are separate matters. Agree their timing with the bank and the notary.

Offers in Limena and the surrounding area

Limena and the belt towns (Vigonza, Rubano) see demand for three-room flats from commuting families. An offer with a deposit, a qualified mortgage and clear deed terms speeds up the seller’s choice. Compare OMI micro-areas: Limena is not the centre of Padova.

Frequently asked questions

What is a purchase offer?

A written offer with price, terms and conditions that binds the parties if accepted.

Is the offer binding?

A deadline to accept alone does not make the offer irrevocable. Article 1329 concerns the commitment to keep it open for a set time. Article 1326 governs the conclusion of the contract when the offeror learns of the acceptance.

Deposit or payment on account?

The confirmatory deposit (article 1385) binds both parties; a payment on account has different protection.

Is a mortgage condition needed?

Recommended: it makes the agreement effective only if the financing is released within the terms.

How long should the acceptance deadline be?

It is negotiable. In a competitive market short deadlines favour the seller.
