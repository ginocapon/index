Documents needed to buy or sell a home in Padova

Build the property and personal file early, and let the notary and technician identify what this particular transaction needs.

Gino Capon · Adapted from the Italian article · Editorial revision 25 November 2026

In short

Ask the notary for the personal document list, including identity, tax code, marital or property regime and any representation. Provide the earlier title and explain inheritance, gift, co-ownership, usufruct or company involvement. A succession declaration alone does not establish that every sale formality has been completed.

Start with the people and ownership

Ask the notary for the personal document list, including identity, tax code, marital or property regime and any representation. Provide the earlier title and explain inheritance, gift, co-ownership, usufruct or company involvement. A succession declaration alone does not establish that every sale formality has been completed.

Organise the technical file

Collect available permits and later works documents, cadastral records and plans, energy certification and system documents. Ask a qualified technician to compare the actual property with the records. A missing permit cannot safely be treated as resolved merely because the buyer knows about it.

Do not confuse different rights and checks

A cadastral update does not by itself cure a planning problem. Likewise, sale of bare ownership and sale of full ownership where a usufruct exists are different transactions. Ask the notary who must sign and which rights are being transferred rather than assuming every holder’s consent is needed for every transfer.

Include the condominium and payments

Request the administrator’s payment statement, accounts and approved works. Have the notary explain liability and any allocation agreed between buyer and seller. Retain the offer or preliminary contract, registration receipt and traceable payment records. A private allocation in the deed does not automatically bind outside creditors.

Set a realistic completion checklist

Record each required item, the person responsible, status and deadline. Share the list with the notary, technician and bank where relevant. Do not promise that a standard number of days will be enough: title questions, technical discrepancies and financing need their own assessment before a deed date is treated as secure.

General information, not individual legal, tax, technical or financial advice. Adapted with editorial corrections. Check the current rules and the documents with qualified advisers before making a commitment.
