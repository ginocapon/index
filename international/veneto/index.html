VENETO

Look beyond a postcard of Veneto.

Veneto includes historic cities, plains, hills, mountains and coastline. For a property search, the useful question is which setting works for your daily life, rather than which destination looks best in a photograph.

How does Veneto fit a property search?

Use Veneto’s different city, town and rural settings to refine your needs, not to assume an investment return. Righetto’s local starting point is Padova and its province. Each property still needs an individual review of location, access, condition, costs and suitability.

City life and smaller towns

The region’s cultural destinations include Padova, Venezia, Verona, Vicenza and Treviso. Their visitor appeal does not establish that a particular home suits your needs or offers a financial return.

03

The Euganean area

Abano and Montegrotto are among the spa destinations near the Euganean Hills. For a home in the wider area, assess the individual location, access, building condition and the work it may need.
