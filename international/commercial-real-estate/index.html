COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE

Begin with the business, then assess the space.

A shop, office or other business property should be considered against the activity it needs to support. Describe those requirements before deciding which premises to view.

COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATESpace.A planning framework, not a completed project

What belongs in a commercial property brief?

State the intended business activity, preferred area, approximate space, budget and essential access or equipment needs. Separate purchase from rental requirements. Suitability for the proposed use and any necessary permissions or alterations need property-specific professional checks before you commit.

THE PAGE AT A GLANCE

Questions worth settling early.

A practical checklist for your discussion, not a fixed promise about the property or outcome.

01

Explain the intended activity

Set out what the business will do, how many people will use the premises and whether customers, deliveries or equipment need access. These details help define the search brief.

02

Compare functional requirements

List the space, access, storage, utilities and parking requirements that are essential to your activity. Keep these separate from preferences about appearance or layout.

03

Distinguish buying from renting

Consider the intended period of occupation, the capital available and the flexibility the business needs. Compare the proposed terms and the responsibilities attached to each option with your advisers.

04

Check suitability before commitment

Do not assume a property is suitable for an activity because a similar business once occupied it. Ask a qualified professional to check the proposed use and any permissions or alterations needed.

05

Make the costs visible

In addition to price or rent, request information about charges, maintenance responsibilities, work and professional costs. Record which figures are confirmed and which remain estimates.

06

Bring a useful first brief

Send the preferred area, intended activity, approximate space and budget, together with any essential access or timing requirements. If you have seen a listing, include its reference code.
